What's New · Memorize This
Trailhunter ≠ TRD Pro
Trailhunter
SR5-Based Overlanding
Old Man Emu suspension · Bronze wheels · Michelin LTX Trail tires · front recovery hooks + underbody protection.
TRD Pro
The Off-Road Flagship
FOX Q3S shocks · Honeycomb paint · Red tow hooks · available ISO-dynamic seats.
Swap those details and the customer notices. Bronze wheels are Trailhunter; red tow hooks + Honeycomb are TRD Pro.

Connected Services · Prius Trials
Opposite Directions
1 yr → 5 yrs
Remote Connect Trial (Longer)
1 yr → 3 mo
Drive Connect Trial (Shorter)
Remote Connect
Remote start, lock/unlock, and the service that powers Digital Key — now a much longer trial.
Drive Connect
Cloud navigation, Intelligent Assistant, Destination Assist — shrinks the other way. If a rep recalls "the trial changed" but not which way, this is where they miss.
Connected Services · 24MM
Latest Toyota Audio Multimedia
AT&T 5G connectivity — 5G, not LTE.
Standard 14-inch screen on both Tundra and Sequoia.
Customizable widgets on a smartphone-like home screen.
Faster "Hey Toyota" embedded Voice Assistant.
New · Standard
Built-In Drive Recorder Dash Cam
Launches with the latest 24MM. Uses the exterior cameras (front/rear, or Panoramic View Monitor if equipped) to capture 20-second clips of manual and triggered events.
Connected Services · Digital Key
Enhanced Digital Key
Now managed in the device's native digital wallet — Apple, Google, or Samsung — not just the Toyota app.
Share and manage access with up to five additional drivers from the wallet.
Uses NFC to keep working for a period of time even if the phone battery is dead.
Easy to Miss
Requires Remote Connect
Digital Key rides on Remote Connect (5G dependent) — not Drive Connect. When a customer asks what keeps phone-as-key working, the answer is Remote Connect, which is exactly why its trial grew to five years on Prius.

Tundra & Sequoia · Trailhunter
New Trailhunter Package
Based on the SR5 grade, at a more accessible price point.
Michelin LTX Trail 265/70R18 tires.
Old Man Emu upgraded suspension.
Front recovery hooks + additional underbody protection.
Multi-Terrain Select, Crawl Control, locking rear differential.
Unique bronze-colored wheels + exclusive badges.
2.4kW inverter on Tundra i-FORCE MAX grades (and Sequoia).
Standard TSS 4.0 and brighter LED lighting across the lineup.
Tundra & Sequoia · TRD Pro
Purpose-Built TRD Pro
Shocks
FOX Enhanced Q3S / QS3
Newly designed, vehicle-tuned and adjustable. Labeled Q3S on Tundra, QS3 on Sequoia — same FOX family, letters just flip.
Color
New Honeycomb Paint
Bold, high-impact color for Tundra and Sequoia TRD Pro.
Accent
Standard Red Tow Hooks
A functional off-road accent — standard on TRD Pro.
Comfort
ISO-Dynamic Seats
Available on Tundra (and Tacoma) to support comfort and stability off-road.
Prius · What's New
2027 Prius
New "Inked" exterior color replaces Midnight Black Metallic — it doesn't sit alongside it.
Dual-zone automatic climate control now standard.
Offered in four grades, including the striking Nightshade Edition.
A conventional hybrid — no plugging in. (The Prius Prime is the separate plug-in.)
Easy to Miss · TRD Pro Colors
Honeycomb vs. Hot Honey
Honeycomb → Tundra, Sequoia, Tacoma.
Hot Honey (a slightly different shade) → 4Runner.
If the customer's looking at a 4Runner, don't call it Honeycomb.
