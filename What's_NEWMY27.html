⚠ The #1 trap this cycle
Trailhunter and TRD Pro are not the same truck. Trailhunter = SR5-based overlanding, Old Man Emu suspension, bronze wheels, Michelin LTX Trail tires. TRD Pro = the hardcore off-roader, FOX Q3S shocks, Honeycomb paint, red tow hooks. Swap those details and the customer notices.

01 · Connected
Connected Services
What changed in trials, multimedia, and Digital Key for 2027.
Prius trial changes — opposite directions
These two move in different directions. That's the whole trap. Get the service and the direction right.
Remote Connect trialLonger
1 year→5 years
Remote Connect (remote start, lock/unlock, and the service that powers Digital Key) now carries a much longer trial on the 2027 Prius.
Drive Connect trial (applicable)Shorter
1 year→3 months
Drive Connect (cloud navigation, Intelligent Assistant, Destination Assist) shrinks the other way. If a rep remembers "the trial changed" but not which way, this is where they miss.
Latest Toyota Audio Multimedia (24MM)
The newest 24MM system arrives on the 2027 Tundra and Sequoia.
  • Runs on AT&T 5G connectivity (5G, not LTE).
  • Standardizes a 14-inch screen on both trucks.
  • Smartphone-like home screen with customizable widgets.
  • Faster "Hey Toyota" embedded Voice Assistant responses.
New: standard Drive Recorder Dash Cam
Built-in Drive RecorderNew · Standard
Launches with the latest 24MM. Uses the exterior cameras (front/rear, or Panoramic View Monitor if equipped) to capture 20-second clips of both manual and triggered events.
Enhanced Digital Key
  • Now managed in the device's native digital wallet — Apple, Google, or Samsung — not just the Toyota app.
  • Share and manage access with up to five additional drivers from the wallet.
  • Uses NFC to keep working for a period of time even if the phone battery is dead.
  • Requires an active Remote Connect trial/subscription (5G dependent) — not Drive Connect.
⚠ Easy to miss
Digital Key rides on Remote Connect. Drive Connect is navigation and assist. When a customer asks "what subscription keeps my phone-as-key working," the answer is Remote Connect — which is exactly why its trial grew to five years on Prius.

02 · Tundra
2027 Tundra
Rugged updated styling, advanced tech, and the all-new Trailhunter package.
New Trailhunter package
Factory-built overlanding, based on the SR5 grade and positioned at a more accessible price point.
  • Michelin LTX Trail 265/70R18 tires
  • Old Man Emu upgraded suspension
  • Front recovery hooks + additional underbody protection
  • Multi-Terrain Select, Crawl Control, locking rear differential
  • Unique bronze-colored wheels and exclusive badges
Purpose-built TRD Pro upgrades
  • Newly designed, Tundra-tuned FOX enhanced Q3S shocks (adjustable)
  • New Honeycomb exterior color
  • Standard red tow hooks
  • Available ISO-dynamic seats for off-road comfort
Also new for 2027
  • Sharper, more purposeful front-end (CALTY design, hexagonal grille theme)
  • Latest 24MM multimedia — standard 14" screen, AT&T 5G, Drive Recorder
  • Standard Toyota Safety Sense 4.0
  • Upgraded grille-mounted LED light bar; available RIGID fog lights
  • 2.4kW inverter on i-FORCE MAX grades
Grade lineup
SR · SR5 · Limited · Platinum · 1794 Edition · TRD Pro · Capstone — plus the available Trailhunter package. Gas twin-turbo V6 or available hybrid i-FORCE MAX. Full specs and pricing announced fall 2026.

2027 Sequoia
Elevated style, advanced technology, and the newly available Trailhunter package.
New Trailhunter package
Same overlanding recipe as Tundra — SR5-based, factory-built trail capability.

⚠ Easy to miss — the shock label
Sequoia's TRD Pro shocks are labeled QS3; Tundra's are labeled Q3S. Same FOX family, vehicle-specific tuning — the letters just flip. Both are correct for their truck, so match the label to the model.
